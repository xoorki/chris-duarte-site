Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This is a personal portfolio website. It's kept intentionally simple, and so is this policy: I collect as little as possible, and nothing I don't need.

What this site does not do

  • No account creation or login
  • No forms that collect personal data
  • No analytics or advertising trackers
  • No selling or sharing of any data with third parties

What is collected

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like virtually any web host, GitHub's servers automatically log basic technical information for every visit, things like IP address, browser type, and requested pages, for security and operational purposes. I don't have access to detailed visitor logs beyond what GitHub Pages provides, and I don't use that information for tracking or profiling.

Third-party services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). Requesting a font causes your browser to contact Google's servers directly, which is subject to Google's privacy policy. No other third-party services are embedded on this site.

Cookies

This site does not use cookies for tracking or advertising. See the Cookie Policy for details on the local storage used to remember the cookie notice.

Your rights

Since this site doesn't collect personal data beyond standard hosting logs, there's little to request access to or deletion of. If you have questions or concerns about privacy on this site, feel free to reach out using the contact details on the Contact section.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated occasionally to reflect changes to the site. The "last updated" date at the top will always reflect the most recent revision.
