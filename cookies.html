Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This site doesn't use cookies for tracking, advertising, or analytics of any kind. There's no ad network, no analytics script, and no third-party tracker here.

Local storage

The one thing this site does store on your device is a small flag in your browser's local storage, used only to remember that you've dismissed the cookie notice banner. This isn't a cookie in the technical sense: it's not sent to any server, it stays entirely on your device, and it's only ever read by this site to decide whether to show the banner again.

Google Fonts

This site loads typefaces from Google Fonts. Google's font delivery for the fonts used here does not set cookies, though your browser does make a direct request to Google's servers to fetch the font files, which exposes your IP address to Google like any other request to a third-party server. See the Privacy Policy for more on this.

Controlling storage

You can clear or block local storage and cookies at any time through your browser's settings. Doing so just means the cookie notice banner may reappear on your next visit.
